Navigation

Topbar, sidebar, line tabs and dropdowns. The sidebar and topbar you are looking at right now are the real components.

Topbar

.admin-topbar is fixed, 48 px tall. Inside: an optional menu button (mobile), a .brand, then actions pushed right with ms-auto. Use .hide-xs to drop labels on phones, .brand-logo for the square mark.

<nav class="admin-topbar">
  <div class="brand me-3"><a href="/"><span class="brand-logo"><i class="ti ti-bolt"></i></span> App</a></div>
  <div class="d-flex align-items-center gap-2 ms-auto"> … </div>
</nav>

There is also .topbar-timer (add .running for the green state) for time-tracking widgets.

Sidebar

.sidebar.sidebar-fixed#sidebar is 240 px on desktop and slides in on mobile (toggle with data-sidebar-toggle on a button and on the .sidebar-backdrop). Mark the current page with .active; .nav-title adds a group heading; .sidebar-footer pins content to the bottom.

<div class="sidebar sidebar-fixed" id="sidebar">
  <ul class="sidebar-nav" data-coreui="navigation">
    <li class="nav-item"><a class="nav-link active" href="/"><i class="nav-icon ti ti-layout-dashboard"></i> Dashboard</a></li>
    <li class="nav-title">Admin</li>
    <li class="nav-item"><a class="nav-link" href="/audit"><i class="nav-icon ti ti-history"></i> Audit log</a></li>
  </ul>
  <div class="sidebar-footer border-top"><a class="nav-link fs-sm" href="/settings"><i class="ti ti-settings"></i> Settings</a></div>
</div>
<div class="sidebar-backdrop" data-sidebar-toggle></div>

Collapsible groups use CoreUI's .nav-group / .nav-group-items markup; child links are indented and muted automatically.
